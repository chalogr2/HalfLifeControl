To provide you with the most accurate medication tracking and safety alerts, we collect the following types of information:

  • check_circle Device Usage: Technical logs to ensure the app notifications fire reliably on your specific device model.

Security is not just a feature; it's our foundation. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (AES-256) to ensure your health data remains private.

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time. HalfLifeControl provides a simple one-click "Export My Data" and "Delete Account" feature within the app settings. We respect and comply with GDPR and HIPAA guidelines where applicable.
